Navigation

  • Click / drag: move the cursor (updates the other views)
  • Wheel or ↑↓: change slice in the hovered view (Shift = ×5)
  • Right-drag or Ctrl+wheel: zoom
  • Middle-drag or Ctrl+drag: pan
  • 1–9: show / hide a nucleus, 0: all
  • F: zoom to thalamus, R: whole volume
Radiological convention (as in ITK-SNAP): the patient's right is on the left of the screen. Files are reoriented to RAS from their header. Nothing is sent online.
